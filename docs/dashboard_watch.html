How the watchlist works

    


The watchlist in one minute


Cards you don't own yet but might buy. Every scheduled run (or ⬇ Scrape now) checks their SNKRDUNK prices with the
same scraper as your collection, saves everything, and works out two scores per card. You add and edit cards in the
watchlist editor, and change every number below on its Settings tab
(needs config_editor.py running).



Two scores — and why they differ


Investment Score (0–100) = how good the card is long term, whatever today's price.
Buy Score (0–100) = how good today's price is as an entry point. A great card at a silly price scores high
on the first and low on the second. Under each ring, "% data" is how much of that score could actually be judged: anything
without data (or without your rating) scores 0 and is shown as "Not evaluated" — never guessed. So a new card scores low and
climbs as history builds up. Open a card's Details to see every point. Each part is worked out on its own scale and then
rescaled to the weight you give it (the weights add up to 100).



Investment Score — 100 points




Character popularity15Your tier list (out of 15): S 15 pts (9 names) · A 11 pts (24 names) · B 7 pts (17 names); a named character not on the list = 3.


Card rarity15Your rarity table, out of 15 (SAR 9, SIR 9, CSR 8, Promo 9, CHR 6, UR 8, HR 8, SR 6, AR 5, IR 5, RR 3, Other 3, MUR 9, FUR 9, RRR 5), + reprint risk
  low +2 / medium +0 / high -3; capped 0–15.


PSA population20Out of 15: up to 8 for a low PSA 10 count (under 500 = 8, under 1,500 = 6, under 5,000 = 5, under 10,000 = 4, under 15,000 = 3, under 25,000 = 2, higher = 1) + up to 7 for slow growth
  (under 1% a month = 7 … 10%+ = 0). Low-pop points are capped at 4 if the card sells under 1 a week and isn't tier S/A.


Sales & liquidity20Out of 20, by sales per week: 14+ = 20, 7+ = 17, 3+ = 13, 1+ = 8, 0.25+ = 4, fewer = 1.


Price fundamentals15Out of 15: long-term trend (1 year, else 6 months, else 2–4 months) up to 8 + price stability (30-day swings) up to 7.


Overseas demand5Your 0–10 rating, converted to these points (e.g. 8/10 = 80% of them).


Artwork10Your 0–10 artwork-quality rating, converted to these points.





Buy Score — 100 points




Price vs target2510%+ below target = full points, at target = 88% … 35%+ above = 0. Uses your target, or an auto target
  (the price only 25% of the last 90 days' prices were at or below; needs 5+ prices over 3+ weeks).


Price vs history20Price vs the 90-day average: 15%+ below = full points … 20%+ above = 0.


Recent trend157-day move judged with sales: a dip while sales hold up = full points, steady = 60%, rising = a third,
  a speculative spike (15%+ in a week) = 0, falling with weaker sales = a quarter.


Sales & liquidity15The liquidity points above, rescaled.


Investment Score15Investment Score as a share of this weight.


Margin of safety10Out of 10: starts at 8; −3 near its 1-year (or 90-day) high, −3 if PSA 10 pop grows 6%+ a month, −2 for big price
  swings, −4 if demand looks weaker, −3 for a very thin market; +2 in the lower third of its 1-year range (needs a year of history).




Cheap for a bad reason: if the price fell 10%+ while sales dropped below 0.5× normal, the price-vs-target and price-vs-history points are halved
— a price drop alone never makes a card a buy.


Buy Score bands: 90–100 exceptional · 80–89 strong · 70–79 attractive entry · 50–69 fair, consider waiting ·
30–49 expensive · 0–29 poor entry or elevated risk.



Labels (the coloured badge)


The label weighs more than the Buy Score alone, checked in this order:


    

  • Overheated market — up 15%+ in a week, or 15%+ above its 90-day average at the top of its range, or a burst of sales while rising.
    • 

  • Price drop with weak demand — down 10%+ while sales fell below 0.5× normal.
    • 

  • Low-liquidity speculative card — under 1 sale(s) a week: may be hard to sell.
    • 

  • Exceptional (Buy 90+, Investment 70+), Strong
  (Buy 80+, Investment 60+) and Potential (Buy 70.0+) buying opportunity —
  all also need 1+ sale(s) a week and PSA 10 growth under 6% a month.
    • 

  • Long-term hold candidate — Investment 70+ but the price isn't attractive yet.
    • 

  • Watching — none of the above.
    • 



The reasons under each card say why it got its label.



Price data


Changes over 24h, 7d, 30d, 90d, 6m and 1y use real saved prices from that time (the tracker's own checks and SNKRDUNK's
price chart); with no price from then they show n/a. Sales counts come from every individual sale the tracker has seen
("≥" means older sales weren't visible yet). The all-time high/low in Details is for context only and never scored (release
hype and old markets would mislead).



Checklist


Details shows a checklist per card: ✓ yes, ✗ no, • information, — not evaluated. It covers popularity, rarity, PSA
(low PSA 10 pop = under 15,000), liquidity, valuation, overseas demand and artwork.
"Checklist %" is how many items could be evaluated.



US demand (eBay)


Each card's Check on eBay link opens eBay.com's sold listings for it (card name + number + "Japanese" + the grade,
or your own search set in the editor). With the Tampermonkey script installed, opening that page counts the sales from the last
30 days and saves High (15+), Medium (4+) or
Low US demand on the card — a guide for your overseas-demand rating, not part of the score. A ⏰ appears when a check is
21+ days old, and the bar at the top lists the cards that are due. When a card's level changes
you get an ntfy alert and it shows in the 🔔 banner.
Keyword searches can catch the wrong card or English versions, and a full page of results shows as "240+" — use the link to check.
eBay is only read when you open the page yourself; nothing scans it automatically.



PSA numbers


Added with your Tampermonkey script: open the card's GemRate (or PSA) page, or press ⏰ Update stale cards to go through every
card not updated in 7 days, one tab at a time. GemRate gives the PSA 10 count, PSA 9 count, all-grades total and gem rate.



Alerts


Sent to your ntfy topic when a checked card's price moves: drop of 8.0%+ in 24h or 15.0%+ in 7 days,
rise of 10.0%+ in 24h or 20.0%+ in 7 days, spike of 25.0%+ in 24h, 30-day trend
±20.0%, a 8.0%+ dip while sales stay strong, your target reached, unusual sales volume, or
Buy Score 80.0+. Everything a card triggers in one check is combined into one message; the same alert never repeats within
24.0h and is re-sent at most every 72.0h while it stays true. The last 72 hours of alerts also show in the
🔔 banner on your PSA10 and raw dashboards. Change any of this on the editor's Settings tab.



Using this page


    

  • Filters and sort at the top (remembered in this browser).
    • 

  • Compare: tick "Compare" on 2–3 cards, then press Compare in the bar at the bottom for a side-by-side view with the
  key differences spelled out and the better value in each row highlighted.
    • 

  • Details on a card: price chart, market data, both score breakdowns, checklist, score history, alerts and your notes.
    • 

  • ⬇ Scrape now checks all watchlist cards on SNKRDUNK now; 🔄 Refresh redraws the pages from saved data

Your data


Kept in config_watch.json and data/watch_*.json — prices, every sale seen, PSA history, score history,
alerts, and every change to targets, ratings and checklists. Nothing is overwritten, and everything is in the daily backup
(py restore_backup.py). Buying a card ("I bought this…" in the editor) can move it into your collection and keeps its
watchlist history. The page is published to GitHub Pages with your other dashboards.



Scores and labels are indicators to support your decisions, not investment advice.
