    Labels. Exceptional (Buy 90+, Investment 70+), Strong (Buy 80+, Investment 60+) and Potential (Buy 80+) buying
    opportunities also need at least 1 sale a week and PSA 10 growth under 6% a month — a price drop alone never makes a card a buy.
    "Price drop with weak demand" = down 10%+ while sales slowed to under half of normal. "Overheated" = up 15%+ in a week, or well above
    its 90-day average at the top of its range. "Low-liquidity" = under 1 sale a week. "Long-term hold" = Investment 70+ but the price isn't
    attractive yet.

    Buy Score bands. 90–100 exceptional · 80–89 strong · 70–79 attractive entry · 50–69 fair, consider waiting · 30–49 expensive ·
    0–29 poor entry or elevated risk.
